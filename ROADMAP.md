# IAFE Roadmap

> **Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)**

**Enterprise Roadmap for Building an Intelligent Knowledge, Innovation and Industrial Ecosystem**

---

# Purpose

This roadmap describes the planned evolution of the IAFE ecosystem from architectural foundations to a fully integrated enterprise platform supporting education, research, industry, governance and society.

The roadmap is iterative and will evolve as new capabilities, technologies and industry requirements emerge.

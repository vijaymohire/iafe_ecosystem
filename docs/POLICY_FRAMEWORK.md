# Policy Framework

> **Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)**

**Enterprise Policy Framework for Intelligent, Secure and Sustainable Ecosystems**

---

# Purpose

The Policy Framework establishes the principles, structures and policy domains that guide decision-making across the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE).

Policies provide consistent direction while allowing organizations to adapt implementation according to their operational, regulatory and business requirements.

The framework promotes responsible governance, trusted collaboration and continuous improvement throughout the ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Policy Framework aims to:

- Establish consistent enterprise policies
- Support organizational governance
- Improve interoperability
- Protect enterprise assets
- Enable trusted collaboration
- Promote responsible innovation
- Improve compliance readiness
- Support sustainable operations
- Encourage continuous learning

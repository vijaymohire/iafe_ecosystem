# IAFE Overview

> **Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)**

**Connecting Knowledge • Innovation • Industry • Society**

---

# Executive Summary

The **Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)** is a federated enterprise framework designed to integrate education, research, innovation, manufacturing, governance and intelligent operations into a continuously improving ecosystem.

Rather than treating education, research and industry as independent domains, IAFE connects them through a shared operating model where knowledge continuously evolves into innovation, innovation becomes products and services, and operational experience is captured and transformed back into reusable knowledge.

The framework provides a practical implementation approach for organizations pursuing **Industry 5.0**, **Society 5.0**, **Digital Transformation**, **Human-Centric Engineering**, **Sustainability**, and **National Self-Reliance** initiatives.

---

# Why IAFE?

Organizations today face several common challenges:

- Knowledge silos
- Skills shortages
- Fragmented innovation
- Underutilized resources
- Limited collaboration
- Supply chain complexity
- Operational inefficiencies
- Governance and compliance challenges

IAFE addresses these challenges by integrating learning, innovation, production and continuous improvement into one intelligent ecosystem.

# Purpose

The QAI Integration Framework describes how the Quantum AI (QAI) Platform integrates with the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE) to provide intelligent orchestration, decision support, automation and continuous optimization.

Rather than replacing existing enterprise systems, QAI augments them by providing intelligent services that improve learning, research, engineering, manufacturing and governance.

---

# Role of QAI

Within IAFE, the QAI Platform acts as the intelligent coordination layer.

Its primary objectives are to:

- Connect distributed enterprise capabilities.
- Improve decision-making.
- Accelerate innovation.
- Optimize resource utilization.
- Enable intelligent collaboration.
- Support continuous learning.

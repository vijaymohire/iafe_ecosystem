# Enterprise Capability Matrix

> **Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)**

**Enterprise Capability Catalog for Intelligent Ecosystems**

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Capability Matrix provides a structured view of the major capabilities within the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE), their responsibilities, stakeholders and interactions.

The matrix serves as a reference for enterprise architects, solution designers, governance teams, developers and ecosystem partners when designing or integrating capabilities.

---

# Capability Overview

The IAFE ecosystem is organized into reusable enterprise capabilities.

```
                 IAFE Ecosystem

                        │

 Academy ─ Innovation ─ Factory

        │         │          │

 Platform ─ Governance ─ Knowledge

        │         │          │

 Products ─ Research ─ Ecosystem
```

Each capability is independently deployable while remaining interoperable through shared interfaces and enterprise standards.

---

# Enterprise Capability Matrix

| Capability | Primary Purpose | Key Outputs | Primary Stakeholders |
|------------|-----------------|-------------|----------------------|
| Academy | Learning and workforce development | Skills, certifications, knowledge | Students, educators, industry |
| Innovation | Research and technology transfer | Prototypes, patents, solutions | Researchers, innovators |
| Factory | Product and service delivery | Products, services, operations | Engineering, manufacturing |
| Platform | Intelligent enterprise services | AI, orchestration, analytics | Developers, operators |
| Products | Commercial and reusable offerings | Platforms, applications, solutions | Customers, partners |
| Knowledge | Enterprise knowledge management | Standards, documentation, knowledge assets | Entire ecosystem |
| Research | Future technology development | Research outputs, validation | Universities, R&D |
| Governance | Trust and enterprise direction | Policies, compliance, architecture | Leadership, governance teams |
| Ecosystem | Federated collaboration | Partnerships, shared services | Governments, industry, academia |

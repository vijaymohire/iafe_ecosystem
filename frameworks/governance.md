# Governance Framework

> **IAFE Governance Framework**

**Building Trusted, Secure and Sustainable Enterprise Ecosystems**

---

# Purpose

The **IAFE Governance Framework** provides a reusable enterprise framework for establishing trust, accountability, transparency and responsible decision-making across the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE).

The framework enables organizations to balance innovation with governance by integrating enterprise architecture, policies, standards, compliance and intelligent decision support.

Rather than restricting innovation, governance provides the foundation that enables organizations to collaborate confidently while protecting people, knowledge and enterprise assets.

---

# Vision

Establish intelligent, transparent and trusted governance that enables sustainable innovation, responsible technology adoption and continuous organizational improvement.

---

# Mission

Provide organizations with a practical governance framework that supports enterprise transformation through consistent policies, architecture, accountability and intelligent oversight.

# FAEP Framework

> **Federated Autonomous Ecosystem Platform (FAEP)**

**The Foundation Framework of the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)**

---

# Purpose

The **Federated Autonomous Ecosystem Platform (FAEP)** provides the foundational enterprise framework that enables intelligent, federated collaboration across education, research, industry, government and society.

FAEP establishes the architectural principles, integration model and operational capabilities required to build continuously learning and continuously improving ecosystems.

Within IAFE, FAEP serves as the ecosystem foundation upon which specialized frameworks such as Academy, Factory, QAI and Governance are built.

---

# Vision

Build an intelligent ecosystem where knowledge, innovation, people, organizations and technology continuously collaborate to improve productivity, sustainability and societal outcomes.

---

# Mission

Provide a reusable enterprise framework that enables organizations to collaborate through federated architectures while preserving autonomy, governance and trusted information exchange.

---

FAEP is founded on the following principles:

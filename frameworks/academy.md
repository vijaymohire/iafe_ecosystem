# Academy Framework

> **IAFE Academy Framework**

**Continuous Learning, Knowledge Development and Workforce Readiness for the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem**

---

# Purpose

The **IAFE Academy Framework** provides a reusable model for developing people, knowledge, skills and research capabilities across the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE).

The Academy connects education with research, innovation and industry so that learning continuously responds to real-world technological, workforce and societal needs.

Rather than treating education as a separate stage that ends before employment, the Academy supports **lifelong learning and continuous capability development** throughout the ecosystem.

---

# Vision

Create an accessible, continuously evolving learning ecosystem where people can acquire, apply and improve knowledge throughout their education, careers and participation in industry and society.

---

# Mission

Provide modular, reusable and industry-connected learning capabilities that accelerate access to emerging technologies, strengthen workforce readiness and enable continuous knowledge exchange between education, research and industry.

---

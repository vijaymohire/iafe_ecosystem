# Knowledge Framework

> **IAFE Knowledge Framework**

**Enterprise Knowledge Management Framework for the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem**

---

# Purpose

The **IAFE Knowledge Framework** provides a reusable enterprise framework for creating, organizing, sharing, protecting and continuously improving knowledge across the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE).

Knowledge is treated as a strategic enterprise asset that connects people, research, engineering, products and operations into a continuously learning ecosystem.

Rather than storing information in isolated repositories, the framework promotes connected, reusable and discoverable knowledge that supports better decisions, innovation and long-term organizational learning.

---

# Vision

Build intelligent knowledge ecosystems where information evolves into understanding, understanding becomes capability and capability creates sustainable value.

---

# Mission

Enable organizations to capture, organize, reuse and continuously improve knowledge through structured architectures, intelligent technologies and collaborative practices.

# Factory Framework

> **IAFE Factory Framework**

**Transforming Knowledge into Products, Services and Sustainable Value**

---

# Purpose

The **IAFE Factory Framework** provides a reusable enterprise framework for transforming knowledge, research, engineering and innovation into products, services, intellectual property and operational capabilities.

Within the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE), the Factory serves as the value realization engine that converts ideas into measurable economic, industrial and societal outcomes.

Rather than focusing solely on manufacturing, the Factory encompasses the complete lifecycle of engineering, production, delivery and continuous improvement.

---

# Vision

Build intelligent factories that continuously transform knowledge into sustainable value through engineering excellence, innovation and intelligent automation.

---

# Mission

Provide organizations with a reusable framework for engineering, producing, operating and continuously improving products and services while maintaining quality, sustainability and customer value.

# Innovation Framework

> **IAFE Innovation Framework**

**Transforming Knowledge into Future Capabilities through Continuous Innovation**

---

# Purpose

The **IAFE Innovation Framework** provides a reusable enterprise framework for discovering, developing, validating and scaling new ideas into sustainable products, services, technologies and societal capabilities.

Within the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE), Innovation connects research, engineering, knowledge and industry to create continuous technological and organizational advancement.

Innovation is treated as an ongoing enterprise capability rather than an isolated research activity.

---

# Vision

Create intelligent innovation ecosystems where ideas continuously evolve into practical solutions that improve organizations, industries and society.

---

# Mission

Accelerate innovation through reusable frameworks, collaborative research, intelligent technologies and continuous knowledge exchange between academia, industry, government and communities.

# Purpose

The **Quantum Artificial Intelligence (QAI) Framework** provides the intelligent orchestration layer for the Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE).

Rather than focusing solely on Artificial Intelligence or Quantum Computing, QAI combines multiple intelligent technologies into a unified enterprise framework that supports learning, research, engineering, manufacturing, governance and continuous ecosystem optimization.

The framework augments human expertise by enabling evidence-based decision support, intelligent automation and adaptive enterprise operations.

---

# Vision

Build intelligent enterprise ecosystems where people, knowledge and technology collaborate continuously to improve innovation, productivity, sustainability and societal outcomes.

---

# Mission

Provide a reusable intelligence framework that enables organizations to integrate Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing and advanced digital technologies into enterprise operations while maintaining responsible governance and human oversight.

# Ecosystem Framework

> **IAFE Ecosystem Framework**

**Building Intelligent, Federated and Continuously Improving Enterprise Ecosystems**

---

# Purpose

The **IAFE Ecosystem Framework** provides a reusable enterprise framework for integrating people, knowledge, organizations, technologies and intelligent platforms into a continuously evolving ecosystem.

Rather than viewing education, research, engineering, manufacturing and governance as separate domains, the Ecosystem Framework connects them through shared enterprise capabilities, trusted collaboration and continuous learning.

The framework enables organizations to work independently while participating in larger regional, national and global ecosystems.

---

# Vision

Create intelligent ecosystems where people, organizations and technology collaborate continuously to improve knowledge, innovation, productivity, sustainability and economic prosperity.

---

# Mission

Enable federated collaboration through reusable enterprise capabilities, intelligent technologies and trusted governance while preserving organizational autonomy and local decision-making.

---

# IAFE Architecture

> **Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)**
>
> Enterprise Architecture for Intelligent Knowledge, Innovation and Industrial Transformation

---

# Purpose

The **Integrated Academy–Factory Ecosystem (IAFE)** provides a federated enterprise architecture that integrates education, research, innovation, manufacturing, governance and intelligent operations into a continuously improving ecosystem.

Unlike traditional linear organizations, IAFE enables knowledge, products and operational experience to continuously reinforce one another through intelligent feedback loops powered by the QAI Platform.
